01. Engineering Case Study

Autonomous Combat Platform

Autonomous 3kg Mega Sumo Combat Robot

Hardware Failure Analysis & Redesign

Engineered an autonomous high-torque combat platform. Under aggressive direction switching, high stall currents from the JSUMO Symtec Q gearmotors caused a catastrophic MOSFET thermal runaway on the original XMotion controller.

⚡ The Engineering Pivot:

Decoupled the power stage from logic processing by introducing an ARM Cortex-M7 Teensy 4.1 (600 MHz) paired with an industrial Dual IFX007T (50A) H-Bridge driver. This solved inductive back-EMF spikes, sustained heavy stall currents, and achieved a zero-lag sensor-to-actuation loop.
